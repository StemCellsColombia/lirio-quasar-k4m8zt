The science

What 50 million cells do inside your knee.

Mesenchymal stem cells act as signaling cells. They release growth factors and cytokines that act on the cartilage, the joint lining and the bone you already have.

The inflamed lining settles

Much knee pain comes from an inflamed synovium. The cells may lower the mediators (IL-1β, TNF-α) that keep it irritated.

Your cartilage cells get support

Their signals may push your chondrocytes to lay down more type II collagen, what cartilage is made of.

Less cartilage breakdown

Worn cartilage releases enzymes (MMPs) that dissolve it. The signaling may damp them down.

A joint that glides

The lining may produce more hyaluronic acid and lubricin, the molecules that let the joint slide.

Every batch, lab-verified

  • Passage 3–4, viability above 90%

  • A certificate of analysis for your own cells

What the cells do

Inside your knee, the cells work on the joint itself.

Mesenchymal stem cells act as signaling cells: they release molecules that may change the environment inside a worn joint.

Calm the inflammation

They release anti-inflammatory cytokines (chemical messengers) that may lower the swelling and pain of an arthritic knee.

Support the cartilage

Their growth factors may support the chondrocytes, the cells that maintain the cushion between your bones.

Placed where the damage is

With ultrasound guidance the doctor sees inside the joint in real time while placing the cells.

The cells come from a lab that follows GMP standards, at passage 3–4, with viability verified before every application.

Questions

What patients ask first.

Am I a candidate?

Most people with knee arthritis, cartilage wear or a meniscus tear can be evaluated. Send your X-ray or MRI and the doctor reviews it with you in the free consultation, before you spend anything.

Does it hurt?

The application is done with local anesthesia, so you stay comfortable. Light sedation can be added to your plan.

When will I notice a change?

Improvement builds gradually, usually between 4 and 6 months after treatment, and some patients keep improving after that. Results vary.

Can both knees be treated on the same trip?

Yes. Both knees can be treated on the same day, and your written quote shows exactly what's included.

How long do I stay in Medellín?

The treatment itself takes one day. The doctors recommend staying about 3 days in Colombia before flying home.

Is it legal?

Stem cell therapy is not approved by the FDA, but in Colombia it is legal. People with active cancer cannot be treated.
